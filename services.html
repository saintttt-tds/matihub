Our Services

Complete construction and drilling solutions, tailored to Zimbabwean conditions

Borehole & Water Well Drilling

Matihub specialises in borehole drilling for residential, agricultural, and institutional clients across Zimbabwe. Using rotary and down-the-hole (DTH) hammer techniques suited to our granite and gneiss geology, we reach depths of up to 150 metres.

  • Domestic and suburban borehole drilling
  • Agricultural and irrigation boreholes
  • Institutional and community water points
  • Borehole rehabilitation, deepening & pump installation

Geotechnical Drilling

Our geotechnical drilling services provide the subsurface information developers need before they build. We carry out soil sampling, rock coring, and in-situ testing to support safe, code-compliant foundation design.

  • Site investigation and soil analysis
  • Rock coring and sampling
  • Standard penetration testing (SPT)
  • Groundwater monitoring well installation

Commercial Construction

From ground-up construction to renovations, Matihub delivers commercial building projects across Harare and beyond. We handle everything from foundations to finishing.

  • Office buildings and retail spaces
  • Industrial warehouses and facilities
  • Structural concrete and steelwork
  • Project management and consulting

Piling & Foundation Systems

We design and install deep foundation systems for challenging soil conditions. Our piling solutions ensure structural stability for buildings, bridges, and heavy infrastructure.

  • Bored cast-in-situ piles
  • Driven piles (steel, concrete, timber)
  • Micro piles for restricted access sites
  • Sheet pile retaining walls
