About Matihub

Building trust through quality construction and drilling — proudly Zimbabwean since 2019

Our Story

Matihub was founded in 2019 in Harare, Zimbabwe, starting out as a small borehole drilling crew serving households and farms around Mashonaland during a period when reliable water access was in short supply. Word of our workmanship travelled fast, and the business grew quickly.

Today, we've become a full-service construction and drilling company serving clients across Harare, Bulawayo, and Manicaland. In just a few years we've completed more than 60 projects, ranging from residential boreholes to commercial construction and civil works.

We operate a growing fleet of drill rigs, excavators, concrete mixers, and support equipment, backed by a team of 35+ professionals including geotechnical technicians, project managers, and certified drill operators.
